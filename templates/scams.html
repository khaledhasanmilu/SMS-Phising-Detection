MOBILE BANKING৳25,000 lost

The Fake bKash Agent

DHAKA · 2023

A caller claimed to be a bKash agent, said the account would be blocked, and asked for the OTP "for verification". The victim shared it — money was gone in minutes.

"bKash: Your account will be SUSPENDED. Verify now with OTP or call 01XXXXXXXXX. — bKash Agent"
✓ Lesson: no real agent ever asks for your OTP or PIN.

BANK PHISHING৳80,000 lost

Account Blocked — Verify Now!

WORLDWIDE · ONGOING

An SMS warned the bank account was "locked" with a link to "verify". The link opened a pixel-perfect fake bank page that stole login + OTP.

"URGENT: Your account has been locked. Verify immediately: http://secure-bank-verify.tk/login"
✓ Lesson: banks never send login links by SMS. Open the app yourself.
