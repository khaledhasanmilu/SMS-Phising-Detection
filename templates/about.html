About

Why We made this

My family kept getting prize messages, fake bank warnings, and unknown links. I wanted one simple place where anyone can check such SMS — so I built it.

Why we made this

Because nobody should lose their savings to a text message.

Every day, thousands of people get SMS that look real — a prize, a bank warning, a parcel fee, a job offer. One tired moment, one quick tap, and the money is gone. Banks can't watch every message, and elders often have nobody to ask. So we made a free second opinion that anyone can use in seconds: paste the message, get a straight answer, and learn the tricks along the way. If this page stops even one person from paying a fraud, it was worth building.

My story

Last year my uncle almost sent money to a "lottery agent" who messaged him on his phone. He stopped only because someone nearby said — wait, check first. That day I realised most people have nobody to ask. So I built PhishGuard: a free page where anyone can paste a doubtful message and get a second opinion in seconds. I use it myself, my family uses it, and now it's here for you too.

What it looks for

Every message is checked for the tricks frauds use most: fake prize promises, rushing words like "urgent", strange or shortened links, and requests for OTPs or PINs. If any of these show up, you'll see exactly which ones — so each check also teaches you what to watch for next time.

No signup. No fees. Nothing of yours is stored. This is a free helper for everyday people — students, parents, shopkeepers — anyone with a phone. If it saves even one person from sending money to a fraud, it did its job.

Good to know

No checker in the world is 100% right, and this one is no exception. Use it as a careful second opinion, not a final judge. My rule is simple: if a message rushes you, promises money, or asks for a code — delete it and tell your family, no matter what any checker says.
