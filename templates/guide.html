Safety guide

How to use this checker

Three small habits that keep your money and accounts safe.

01

Copy the message

Got an SMS about a prize, a blocked account, a parcel, or a job? Long-press it on your phone and copy the full text.

02

Paste it in the checker

Open the Scanner page, paste the message in the box, and press Scan. You'll get the answer in seconds.

03

Do what it says

If it says safe — relax. If it says scam — delete the SMS, block the number, and warn your family members too.

Warning signs

4 tricks scammers use

Fake prizes

"You WON!", "FREE phone", "claim your gift" — if you never joined anything, the prize is fake.

Rushing you

"URGENT", "last chance", "account will be blocked today" — they rush you so you don't think.

Strange links

Short or odd-looking links hide the real website. Never tap them directly — check here first.

Asking for codes

"Send your OTP to verify" — no real bank, office, or courier ever asks for your OTP or PIN.

Stay safe

What to do

1. Stop and check

Paste the SMS into PhishGuard before clicking, replying, or paying anything.

2. Call the official number

Worried about your bank or parcel? Call their real helpline or open their official app — never the number from the SMS.

3. Never share OTPs

Your OTP and PIN are only for you. Anyone asking for them is a fraud, even if they sound polite.

4. Block and tell others

Delete the message, block the sender, and show it to your parents and friends so they don't fall for it.

Questions

Common questions

Is the checker always right?

It catches most common scams, but no checker is perfect. Rule of thumb: if a message rushes you, promises money, or asks for a code — treat it as a scam even if you're unsure.

Are my messages saved anywhere?

No. Nothing is stored or sent to anyone. Your check counter resets whenever you press reset or close the browser.

Is it free? Do I need an account?

Yes, free — and no account needed. Just open the page and use it, on phone or computer.
